CALCULATION METHOD

Simple enough to audit.

GradeLens uses the values already printed on a transcript. It does not infer quality points from marks or invent a university policy.

  1. ValidateCredit hours and quality points are checked for impossible values.
  2. AggregateCGPA = total counted quality points ÷ total counted credit hours.
  3. CompareRepeat rules are clearly labelled as scenarios so you can see their effect.
